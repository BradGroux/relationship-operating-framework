## Executive summary

The candidate resists the tested transactional, manipulative, invasive, scored,
autonomous, sales, private-data, and cross-framework misuse paths. Human
ownership does not override consent, privacy, actual authority, access controls,
or another person's agency.

The contribution templates warn against publishing private context, require
scope and evidence distinctions, route sensitive reports privately, and do not
grant normative authority. Repository validation and release checks do not
contact, score, classify, or transact with people.
