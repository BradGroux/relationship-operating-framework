## Executive summary

A new practitioner can navigate from purpose and authority to practical
guidance and fictional examples without prior project context. Re-engagement,
handoff, missed-commitment, and bounded-assistance exercises each produce an
accountable choice while preserving wait, repair, closure, no action, and do not
contact as valid outcomes.

The new repository and release surfaces create explicit contributor duties, not
hidden requirements for practicing Relationship. The method remains usable in
ordinary prose without software, scoring, schemas, or a prescribed lifecycle.
