## Executive summary

The candidate resisted every tested misuse path. It does not authorize a sales
funnel, scoring system, reciprocity tactic, inferred intimacy, scraped
relationship intelligence, autonomous outreach, technical conformance system,
coercive repair, organizational appropriation, or absorption of the Influence
Operating Framework.

Human ownership is necessary but not sufficient: the accountable person remains
bounded by consent, privacy, policy, access controls, actual authority, and the
other person's agency.

## Adversarial evidence

- **Sales and scoring:** Sales stages, scores, conversion models, and ranking by
  predicted value are outside scope (`framework/charter.md:77-85`;
  `framework/principles-and-boundaries.md:100-108`).
- **Contribution for access:** Contribution creates no right to attention,
  access, response, referral, sale, or relationship
  (`framework/principles-and-boundaries.md:38-46`).
- **Inferred intimacy or consent:** Profiles and proximity do not establish
  closeness; consent is current, specific, and controlled by the affected
  person (`framework/principles-and-boundaries.md:9-17`;
  `framework/principles-and-boundaries.md:59-67`).
- **Scraping and enrichment:** Scraping, enrichment, surveillance, inferred
  intimacy, covert personalization, and mass outreach are prohibited
  (`framework/charter.md:82-84`; `framework/practice-guide.md:136-144`).
- **Autonomous or batch outreach:** Agents cannot choose recipients or channels,
  send, schedule, queue, retry, or treat batch or past approval as authority
  (`framework/practice-guide.md:140-148`;
  `examples/03-assisted-commitment-follow-through.md:39-55`).
- **Technical system drift:** CRM, schemas, protocols, runtimes, automation, and
  conformance layers are explicit anti-scope (`framework/charter.md:77-88`).
- **Coercive repair:** Repair cannot demand engagement, forgiveness, restored
  access, or renewed contact (`framework/practice-guide.md:95-113`).
- **Organizational appropriation:** A private relationship does not
  automatically belong to an organization; a handoff transfers only authorized
  necessary context (`framework/practice-guide.md:115-129`).
- **Influence absorption:** Relationship retains a distinct subject and does not
  inherit Influence's concerns, moves, outcome model, or community-growth
  method (`framework/charter.md:93-107`).
