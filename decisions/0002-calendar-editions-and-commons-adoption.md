## Rationale, conflicts and alternatives

The old charter's contribution-before-asks wording could make support seem
conditional. Commons' new principle explicitly permits help without prior
contribution. Adopt only with the charter and practice corrected; do not treat
the previous wording as already equivalent. Retaining the old pin would have
been legitimate but would leave that ambiguity unresolved. No shared-principle
change or local deviation is needed. The owner authorized this bounded local
remediation, adoption and release; independent review remains required.

The changes express chosen non-coercive commitments. They are not empirical
claims about how trust develops. The strongest counterexamples are a person
with no capacity to contribute asking for help, a promised delivery after
contact permission is withdrawn, and a repair request that would impose more
harm. These require honest help-seeking, restraint and bounded handling of
remaining duties rather than earned access or compulsory continuity.

## Affected guidance and independent scope

Charter, principles, practice, glossary references, README, contribution and
governance now cite the exact shared edition. Governance adopts bounded
conflict handling and explicit adoption with exceptions; release operations
use date identifiers and separately described compatibility. The local review
process already separates examples from evidence and keeps authority human.

Commons' principles, boundaries, governance, research/review guidance, Decision
0002, adverse cases, audit disposition and release procedure were considered.
Its repository implementation and protection settings are Commons-local, not
shared obligations. Relationship retains its own small validation surface and
imports no package metadata, schema, workflow or research quota.

Influence's cited v1.0.0 charter concerns contribution and participation;
Focus's charter concerns individual attention and private practice. Neither
method is adopted. Relationship retains stewardship across time without a sales
funnel, productivity requirement or authority to inspect private journals. No
other product adoption or release changes. Wider ecosystem implementations
have no material integration with this repository and need no changes here.

## Compatibility, dissent and reconsideration

This is substantive and potentially incompatible with interpretations that
require earned help, permit channel switching after silence, prioritize old
promises over boundaries or require exhaustive notes. Adopters should review
those decisions; no data migration or package-version change exists.

No substantive dissent has been recorded. No practitioner or specialist field
evidence was supplied, and fictional review does not fill that gap. The steward
should revisit the guidance when consented real use exposes continuing pressure,
unsafe stopping, misunderstood retention or excessive burden. Do not initiate
research, contact participants or collect private context under this decision.
