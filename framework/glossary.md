# Glossary

This glossary defines only terms used with a specific meaning in the
Relationship Operating Framework. Shared ecosystem vocabulary remains in [Open
Framework Commons v1.1.0](https://github.com/BradGroux/open-framework-commons/blob/v1.1.0/CONTEXT.md).

## Agent

A tool-assisted participant that may prepare, organize, compare, draft, or
reflect within a human-approved scope. An agent is not a relationship owner and
has no independent authority to contact, score, manipulate, promise, or
transact with people.

## Ask

A request for another person's time, attention, access, introduction,
endorsement, work, information, decision, or commercial participation.

## Commitment

An explicit promise or accepted responsibility that a person or team is
accountable for honoring, renegotiating, repairing, or closing. A reminder or
machine inference is not itself proof of a commitment.

## Consent

Current, specific permission controlled by the affected person. Consent applies
to its stated context and can be limited or withdrawn.

## Context

The minimum relevant facts, statements, history, uncertainty, commitments, and
boundaries needed for responsible stewardship. Context is not permission to
collect everything available about a person.

## Continuity

The responsible preservation of enough shared understanding, commitments, and
boundaries for stewardship to continue across time, quiet periods, role
changes, and authorized handoffs.

## Contribution

Useful help, work, knowledge, access, or care offered without creating a debt or
entitlement to reciprocity.

## Do not contact

A decision or boundary that external contact must not occur. It must travel
across authorized team handoffs and tools so a new person or system does not
restart unwanted communication.

## Re-engagement

An honest invitation to resume contact after a period of quiet or changed
circumstances. Past familiarity does not guarantee present permission, access,
or response.

## Relationship

A human reality shaped by actual shared experience, trust, commitments,
consent, boundaries, and change. Information about a relationship is not the
relationship itself.

## Relationship owner

A named person accountable for stewardship decisions and external actions. The
term describes responsibility for practice; it never means ownership of another
person.

## Repair

A proportionate response to a missed commitment, misunderstanding, or harm that
acknowledges what occurred, respects the affected person's agency, and does not
demand forgiveness or restored access.

## Stewardship

The accountable care of continuity, commitments, consent, context, boundaries,
repair, and learning across the life of a relationship.
